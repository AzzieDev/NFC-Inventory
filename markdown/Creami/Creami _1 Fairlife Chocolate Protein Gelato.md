# Fairlife Chocolate Protein Gelato

## Ingredients
- 1½ cups Fairlife 2% chocolate milk  
- 1 scoop vanilla whey protein  
- 2 tbsp sugar-free chocolate caramel Coffee Mate  
- 1½ tbsp sugar-free vanilla Jell-O  
- Pinch of salt  
- Sweetener to taste  

## Instructions
1. Add Fairlife chocolate milk to a bowl.  
2. Slowly whisk in the vanilla whey protein until fully dissolved.  
3. Add sugar-free chocolate caramel Coffee Mate.  
4. Add sugar-free vanilla Jell-O pudding mix and whisk until smooth.  
5. Add sweetener and a pinch of salt.  
6. Pour into Ninja Creami pint to the MAX FILL line (≈ 14 oz).  
7. Freeze for 24 hours.  
8. Run Gelato or Ice Cream cycle.  
9. Re-spin once if needed.
