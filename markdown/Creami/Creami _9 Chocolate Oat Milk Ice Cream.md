# Chocolate Oat Milk Ice Cream

## Ingredients
*   **Oat Milk:** 5 oz Planet Oat Sweetened Oat Milk
*   **Dairy Base:** 11 oz Fairlife 2% Chocolate Milk (fill to MAX line)
*   **Stabilizer:** 1 tbsp Sugar-Free Vanilla Jell-O Pudding Mix (or Chocolate/Fudge)
*   **Flavor Enhancer:** 1 pinch of salt

## Instructions
1.  Pour the 5 oz of sweetened oat milk into the Ninja Creami pint.
2.  Pour in the 11 oz of Fairlife 2% Chocolate Milk (this should bring you right up to the MAX FILL line).
3.  Sprinkle the 1 tbsp of sugar-free Jell-O pudding mix and a pinch of salt evenly over the top.
4.  Whisk vigorously or use a milk frother until the pudding powder is completely dissolved into the milk.
5.  Freeze on a level surface for 24 hours.
6.  Run on the **Lite Ice Cream** cycle.
7.  Re-spin once (or twice) if the texture appears crumbly, adding a tiny splash of extra Fairlife or oat milk if needed.
