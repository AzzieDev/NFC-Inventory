### Caramel Macchiato Protein Gelato (Max Fill)

**Ingredients**
* 7 oz Barissimo Cafe Caramel Macchiato
* 7 oz whole milk
* 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
* Pinch of salt

**Instructions**
1. **The Liquid Base:** Pour about 3 oz of the Barissimo Caramel Macchiato into the bottom of the Creami pint.
2. **The Buffer Method:** Dump the scoop of vanilla protein powder and the pinch of salt directly into that small amount of coffee. Vigorously whisk or stir this until it forms a smooth, thick paste. (Using the powder as your new "dry" ingredient in the buffer method prevents dry protein pockets).
3. **The Fill:** Pour in the remaining 4 oz of Caramel Macchiato and the 7 oz of whole milk.
4. **Spoon-Mix:** Gently stir everything together until the thick protein paste is completely dissolved into the surrounding liquids.
5. **Volume Check:** Verify the mixture is sitting at or just below the 14 oz MAX FILL line. 
6. **Freeze:** Place the pint on a level surface in the freezer for 24 hours.
7. **Process:** Lock the pint into the machine and run the **Lite Ice Cream** cycle. 

*(Note: If you still prefer the 3-5 second blender pulse over spoon-mixing, that works perfectly here to instantly hydrate the protein powder. Just let the pitcher sit for 60 seconds before pouring so any resulting foam settles).*
