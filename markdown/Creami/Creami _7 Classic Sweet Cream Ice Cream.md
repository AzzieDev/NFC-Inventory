# Classic Sweet Cream Ice Cream

## Ingredients
*   **Sweetened Condensed Milk:** 3 oz (approx. 6 tbsp)
*   **Dairy Base:** 5 oz Whole Milk
*   **Dairy Alternative:** 5.5 oz Unsweetened Creamy Oat Milk
*   **Stabilizer & Flavor:** 1 tbsp Sugar-Free Vanilla Jell-O Pudding Mix
*   **Flavor Enhancer:** 1 pinch of salt

## Instructions
1.  Add the 3 oz of sweetened condensed milk to the Ninja Creami pint.
2.  Pour in the 5 oz of Whole Milk and the 5.5 oz of Unsweetened Oat Milk. (This brings the total volume to roughly 13.5 oz, resting safely below the MAX FILL line).
3.  Sprinkle the 1 tbsp of sugar-free vanilla pudding mix and the pinch of salt evenly over the top.
4.  Whisk vigorously until the condensed milk is fully incorporated with the lighter milks and the pudding powder is completely dissolved.
5.  Freeze for 24 hours.
6.  Run on the **Lite Ice Cream** cycle.
7.  *(Optional)* Run a dry re-spin if it needs a slightly softer texture, or use the "Mix-In" cycle to add any custom toppings.
