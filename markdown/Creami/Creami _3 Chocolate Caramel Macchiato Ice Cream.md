# Chocolate Caramel Macchiato Ice Cream

## Ingredients
- 2 Coffee Chobani yogurts (5.3 oz each)  
- 1/4 cup Fairlife 2% chocolate milk  
- 2 tbsp sugar-free chocolate caramel Coffee Mate  
- 1 tbsp sugar-free vanilla Jell-O pudding mix  
- 1 packet aspartame  
- Pinch of salt  

## Instructions
1. Add the Coffee Chobani yogurts to a bowl.  
2. Slowly whisk in the Fairlife chocolate milk until combined.  
3. Add sugar-free chocolate caramel Coffee Mate.  
4. Sprinkle in the sugar-free vanilla Jell-O pudding mix and whisk vigorously until fully dissolved.  
5. Add aspartame and a pinch of salt.  
6. Pour into Ninja Creami pint (should sit comfortably below the MAX FILL line ≈ 14 oz).  
7. Freeze for 24 hours.  
8. Run Lite Ice Cream cycle.  
9. Re-spin once with a splash of Fairlife if it looks crumbly.
