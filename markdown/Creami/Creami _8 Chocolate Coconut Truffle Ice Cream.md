# Chocolate Coconut Truffle Ice Cream

## Ingredients
*   **Sweetened Condensed Milk:** 3 oz (approx. 6 tbsp)
*   **Greek Yogurt:** 2 containers (5.3 oz each) Chobani Zero Sugar Toasted Coconut Vanilla (drained/partially scraped)
*   **Liquid Base:** 2 oz (1/4 cup) Fairlife 2% Chocolate Milk
*   **Stabilizer:** 1 tbsp Sugar-Free Vanilla Jell-O Pudding Mix
*   **Flavor Enhancer:** 1 pinch of salt

## Instructions
1.  Add the 3 oz of sweetened condensed milk to the Ninja Creami pint.
2.  Add both drained containers of the Chobani Toasted Coconut Vanilla yogurt.
3.  Pour in the 2 oz of Fairlife Chocolate Milk to thin the mixture. 
4.  Sprinkle the 1 tbsp of sugar-free vanilla pudding mix and the pinch of salt evenly over the top.
5.  Whisk vigorously until the pudding powder is completely dissolved and the chocolate milk is fully integrated with the yogurt and condensed milk.
6.  Ensure the mixture sits comfortably below the MAX FILL line (approx. 13-13.5 oz total volume).
7.  Freeze for 24 hours.
8.  Run on the **Lite Ice Cream** cycle.
9.  *(Optional)* Re-spin with a small splash of Fairlife or oat milk if the texture is crumbly.
