# Double Chocolate Sweet Cream Ice Cream

## Ingredients
*   **Sweetened Condensed Milk:** 3 oz (approx. 6 tbsp)
*   **Liquid Base:** 10.5 oz Fairlife 2% Chocolate Milk
*   **Stabilizer:** 1 tbsp Sugar-Free Vanilla Jell-O Pudding Mix
*   **Flavor Enhancer:** 1 pinch of salt

## Instructions
1.  Add the 3 oz of sweetened condensed milk to the Ninja Creami pint.
2.  Pour in the 10.5 oz of Fairlife Chocolate milk (this should bring you right below the MAX FILL line, hitting about 13.5 oz total).
3.  Sprinkle the 1 tbsp of sugar-free vanilla pudding mix and the pinch of salt evenly over the top.
4.  Whisk vigorously until the pudding powder and thick condensed milk are completely dissolved and integrated into the chocolate milk.
5.  Freeze for 24 hours.
6.  Run on the **Lite Ice Cream** cycle.
7.  *(Optional)* Re-spin with a small splash of Fairlife chocolate milk if the texture comes out crumbly.
