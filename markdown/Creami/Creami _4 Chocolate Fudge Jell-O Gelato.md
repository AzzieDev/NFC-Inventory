# Chocolate Fudge Jell-O Gelato

## Ingredients
- 8 oz prepared Chocolate Fudge Jell-O (made with whole milk)
- 4 oz Fairlife 2% chocolate milk

## Instructions
1. Add the prepared Chocolate Fudge Jell-O directly into your Ninja Creami pint.
2. Pour the Fairlife 2% chocolate milk over the Jell-O.
3. Stir the mixture gently to combine.
4. Ensure the mixture is sitting level in the pint and does not exceed the MAX FILL line.
5. Freeze on a completely level surface for 24 hours.
6. Secure the pint into the outer bowl and run the **Gelato** cycle.
7. Re-spin once if the texture appears crumbly or needs to be a bit smoother.
