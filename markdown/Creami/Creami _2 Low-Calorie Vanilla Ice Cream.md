# Low-Calorie Vanilla Ice Cream

## Ingredients (fills to MAX line ≈ 14 oz)
- 1 cup whole milk  
- 1/2 cup oat milk (sweetened or unsweetened)  
- 1 tbsp sugar-free vanilla Jell-O pudding mix  
- 1 tbsp French vanilla Coffee Mate (not sugar-free)  
- Sweetener to taste (aspartame or preferred)  
- Pinch of salt  
- Optional: 1/2 tsp vanilla extract  

## Instructions
1. Add whole milk + oat milk to a bowl.  
2. Sprinkle in the sugar-free vanilla Jell-O pudding mix while whisking until fully dissolved.  
3. Add French vanilla Coffee Mate, sweetener, salt, and vanilla extract (if using).  
4. Whisk until smooth — do not blend.  
5. Pour into Ninja Creami pint to the MAX FILL line (≈ 14 oz).  
6. Freeze for 24 hours.  
7. Run Ice Cream cycle.  
8. Re-spin once if needed.
